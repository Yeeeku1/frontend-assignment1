Task 5: Midterm Project Topic & Wireframe

Project Description

Our midterm project will focus on developing a Student Resource Hub for IT students. The website aims to centralize course materials, study tools, and student community support in one accessible location. The primary target audience consists of undergraduate students and academic tutors looking for collaborative tools. The interface will feature clean navigation, high responsiveness, and structured content layouts.

Sitemap Structure

The site consists of 5 main pages: Home, Courses, Resource Library, Discussion Board, and Contact Us.

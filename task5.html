Project Description

Study Space is a simple website for university students who want to organize learning resources and study routines. Its target users are students who need a clear place to find notes, track tasks, and discover study tips. The website will make planning easier through a calm, organized interface. It will include pages for the home screen, resources, planner, tips, and contact information.

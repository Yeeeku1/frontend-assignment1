Alan Turing

1912 – 1954 | Father of Modern Computer Science

About Alan Turing

Alan Turing was a pioneering English mathematician, computer scientist, and codebreaker. He played a crucial role in cracking intercepted coded messages during World War II, saving millions of lives. His theoretical concepts laid the groundwork for modern digital computing and artificial intelligence.

Key Life Events

  1. 1912: Born in Maida Vale, London.
  2. 1936: Published paper introducing the universal "Turing Machine" concept.
  3. 1939-1945: Worked at Bletchley Park breaking Enigma ciphers.
  4. 1950: Proposed the Turing Test for Artificial Intelligence.

Major Inventions & Contributions

  • Concept of the Universal Turing Machine
  • Bombe Search Machine used for decryption
  • Design of Automatic Computing Engine (ACE)
  • Foundations of Artificial Intelligence (Turing Test)

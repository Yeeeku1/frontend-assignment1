About Ada Lovelace

Ada Lovelace was an English mathematician, born in 1815 and died in 1852. She is widely known for her notes about Charles Babbage's Analytical Engine. Her notes included an algorithm intended for the machine, which is why she is often described as the first computer programmer. Her work showed that computers could process more than numbers.

Important events

  1. 1815: Ada Lovelace was born in London.
  2. 1833: She met inventor Charles Babbage.
  3. 1843: She published notes on the Analytical Engine.
  4. 1843: Her notes described an algorithm for the machine.
  5. 1953: Her notes were republished, inspiring later computer scientists.

Key contributions

  • Early computer algorithm
  • Vision of general-purpose computing
  • Analytical Engine notes
  • Inspiration for women in STEM
